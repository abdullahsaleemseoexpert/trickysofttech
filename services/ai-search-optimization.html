Common Questions

AI Search Optimization, answered.

What's the difference between AI Search and traditional SEO?
Traditional SEO targets ranking positions on a search results page. AI Search Optimization targets being cited or summarized inside AI-generated answers, which follow different sourcing and trust signals.
Which AI platforms do you track?
ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, Google Gemini, and Google AI Overviews are our core tracked platforms, expanded per client based on where their buyers actually search.
Can you guarantee my brand appears in ChatGPT?
No one can guarantee inclusion in any AI platform's output, and we won't claim otherwise. What we deliver is a documented process that measurably increases citation rate over time.
How do you measure AI visibility?
We run a fixed set of target queries against each platform monthly and record whether, and how accurately, your brand is cited, giving you a trackable citation rate over time.
